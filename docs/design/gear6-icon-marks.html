The tiles Gear6 will sit beside are objects — a lamp, a compass, a knight, a dial. Not one of them spells its own name. Six wordless marks below, each one a thing rather than a label, built on the same four-layer glass.

The shift

From spelling the name to standing for it

A wordmark tile asks to be read before it can be recognised, and at 32px in a dock it never gets read. A silhouette is recognised at a glance, from the corner of the eye, at any size. The G6 lettering stays where it works: the sidebar, the login screen, the site.

Shipping today

Two letterforms on flat #2451B8. Clear, ownable, and doing the same job as the app's name written directly underneath it in the dock.

Proposed

  • SHAPEA silhouette survives blur, distance and 16px. Two glyphs do not.
  • ROOMSymbols can be lit, shadowed and refracted. Letters fight every effect you put on them.
  • FITSitting among objects, a word is the odd one out — it reads as a placeholder.

Six marks

Three take the name literally, three take the product

Same glass build under all of them — only the ground paint and the glyph change. All six on the identical wallpaper so the comparison is honest.

Shift Gate

A six-speed shift pattern with the knob parked in the bottom-right slot — which is where sixth actually lives. It is the name, drawn as a mechanism, and it is the only mark here that could not belong to any other product.

Recommended

Redline

A tachometer with the needle swung into the amber. Instrument, not illustration — graphite glass with one hot arc is the whole thing. The amber tip doubles as the only warm pixel Gear6 owns.

Instrument register

Aperture

Six blades closing on a hexagonal pupil, sat on a six-facet ground so the glyph and the tile agree. Says precision and says six without counting anything out loud. The busiest mark of the set — watch it below 32px.

Optical / precision

Braid

Two strands crossing, one clearly passing under the other — a thread, in the sense the product means it. On clear glass the tile takes its colour from the wallpaper, so the mark is the only fixed thing about the icon.

Threads / conversation

Pulse

Six bars at six heights — channels with traffic in them. The simplest silhouette of the set and the one that holds together smallest, though a meter is common enough that it wins less attention than it earns.

Activity / most legible

Prism

A six-sided solid drawn with three faces showing. Polarity flipped — pale tile, blue mark — because almost every icon in a modern dock is dark, so the light one is found first. The mark thins out below 24px.

Anatomy

Four layers, bottom to top

Stacking order is the whole trick. Move the specular pass below the glyph and the icon instantly reads as a flat sticker again.

  1. GroundThe diagonal gradient. Carries every drop of brand colour; nothing above it is allowed to introduce a new hue.
  2. Light sourceA single off-centre radial at roughly 24% / 6%. One light, one direction, across every icon in the family.
  3. Glyph9-unit stroke on a 100-unit grid, 56% of the tile wide, with a shadow beneath and a one-pixel light lip on top so it sits in the material.
  4. Specular + rimScreen-blended sheen over everything, plus the bright top edge and dim bottom edge that define the slab's thickness.
